Open LLM Leaderboard v2 (OLv2) contributes 26,790 records (52.4% of the dataset) across 4,465 models, yet its population is largely isolated from other sources. Of these models, 4,344 (97.3%) appear exclusively in OLv2 and are evaluated on none of the other 12 platforms. The maximum Jaccard similarity between OLv2's model set and any other source is 0.010, confirming near-total population disjointness. Removing OLv2 reduces the dataset from 51,094 to 24,304 records. While OLv2 contributes high harness and n-shot coverage (70.5% and 55.5% with OLv2 vs 37.9% and 6.5% without), it shares the ecosystem-wide gap in temperature and prompt template reporting (both near 0%). Crucially, OLv2's dominant volume masks the weaker metadata norms of other sources: excluding it reveals that non-OLv2 sources achieve only 37.9% harness coverage and 6.5% n-shot coverage. These findings elevate the OLv2 exclusion from a sensitivity check to a primary structural finding: OLv2 operates as a parallel evaluation ecosystem with minimal population overlap, distinct benchmark scope, and volume that dominates aggregate statistics.
